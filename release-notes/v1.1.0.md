## Vault 1.1.0 — Touch ID unlock

### New
- **Unlock with Touch ID:** turn it on in Settings and the lock screen shows a fingerprint button. The vault key is stored in your macOS Keychain and released only after a successful fingerprint check.
- **Quit on close:** closing the window now quits Vault completely; the vault stays encrypted at rest.

### Under the hood
- Key-based encrypt/decrypt refactor keeps the vault salt stable, so password unlock keeps working after Touch ID is enabled.

### Download
`Vault-1.1.0-arm64.dmg` (drag to Applications) or `Vault-1.1.0-arm64-mac.zip`. Apple Silicon.
